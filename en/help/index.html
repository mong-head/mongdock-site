FAQ

Getting started

I can't see the Dock.

On a new install, the Dock stays hidden until you move the pointer to the edge of the screen. To keep it on screen, choose “Always show” in Settings → Dock.

I can't see the taskbar.

mongdock hides the Windows taskbar while it's running. Turn off Settings → General → “Hide Windows taskbar”, or click the mongdock icon in the notification area once to pause. If mongdock quit unexpectedly and the taskbar is still hidden, start mongdock again, or sign out and back in.

It feels slow over remote desktop.

Turn on Settings → General → “Lightweight mode” to switch off animations and blur.

Can I use it on a work PC?

It installs and runs without administrator rights, but company policies may block hiding the taskbar, starting at sign-in, or downloading calendars. Check your company's rules first.

Routines and All Apps

How do I make a routine?

Open the apps you want and place them where you like. Then right-click an empty spot in the Dock → “Save Current Screen as Routine…”. You can also start a new routine in Settings → Dock → Routines and add apps, links, and games one by one. To finish, right-click the routine icon → “End Routine”. Only the windows the routine opened are closed.

Do routines open by themselves?

By default, a routine opens only when you click it. Turn on a start condition (headset or display connected, PC turned on, a set time, and more), and a banner at the top right asks whether to open it. mongdock asks at most once a day for the same condition.

Can I turn off routine suggestions?

Yes, in Settings → Dock → Routines. They also turn off on their own if you decline three times in a row. mongdock doesn't suggest anything in your first 3 days, and after that asks at most once a week.

What happens when I uninstall sleeping apps?

In the All Apps panel, open “Sleeping apps”, pick the apps, and click “Uninstall in Windows…”. A confirmation card comes first. Then each app's Windows uninstaller opens in turn; finish there and the app is removed. If you'd rather keep an app, “Hide Selected” just hides it from the panel. Bring hidden apps back from “Hidden Apps”.

Notifications and calendar

KakaoTalk notifications don't show as banners.

KakaoTalk uses its own pop-ups instead of Windows notifications, so mongdock can't show them as banners. The red dot on its Dock icon still appears.

Can I connect Naver Calendar?

Personal Naver Calendar doesn't offer an iCal subscription address. For Google Calendar or Outlook.com, copy the iCal address and click Settings → Calendar → “Add from clipboard”.

Privacy, purchase, and removal

Where are my app history and window titles kept?

Only on your PC. The launch history used for routine suggestions keeps just the app and the time, for 14 days. Window titles are counted in memory and never saved. mongdock never reads your browser's address bar or history. To stop and clear this history, right-click an empty spot in the All Apps panel and turn off “Remember apps I use (for suggestions and tidying)”. Usage statistics are sent only if you agree when first asked, and only as counts.

What happens when the trial ends?

After 15 days, the Dock and menu bar stop and the Windows taskbar comes back. Buy mongdock once to pick up where you left off, with your settings intact. No subscription, no ads.

Anything to do before uninstalling?

Go to Settings → About → “Uninstall mongdock…”. It restores the taskbar, auto-hide, and notification sound, and opens the Windows uninstall page.

Routine launch options

Text in Routine editor → app item ▸ → “Advanced: launch options” is passed to the app as-is. Most people can leave it empty.

  • --new-window — open the browser in a new window (from 0.6.1 routine browsers always do)
  • --profile-directory="Profile 1" — open a specific Chrome, Edge or Whale profile (from 0.6.1 just pick it under “Profile”)
  • -n — new VS Code window

Each app accepts different options; see that app's help.

Good to know

  • Apps that use their own pop-ups instead of Windows notifications, such as KakaoTalk, don't appear as banners. The red dot on their Dock icon still shows.
  • After you change the notification sound for the first time, sign out and back in for it to take effect.
  • Naver Calendar can't be connected because it doesn't offer an iCal subscription address.
  • When you end a routine, windows asking whether to save are left open.
  • If mongdock restarts after you open a routine, it can't tell which windows the routine opened, so close them yourself.
  • Apps that only run one window (like KakaoTalk or Discord) aren't opened again if they're already running. mongdock tells you which desktop they're on.
  • Uninstalling sleeping apps opens each app's Windows uninstaller in turn. Apps that can't be uninstalled this way are marked in the list.
  • On Windows 10, rounded corners and some icons may look different from Windows 11.
  • x64 PCs only.

Report a problem

“Report a Problem…” in Settings → About is the quickest way. Attach screenshots or files, and review exactly what's included before you send. Emails, web addresses, user names, and window titles are masked first.

Uninstalling mongdock

Go to Settings → About → “Uninstall mongdock…”. It restores your taskbar, auto-hide, and notification sound, then opens the Windows uninstall page.
