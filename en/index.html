A menu bar on top with app menus, Control Center and a calendar. A Dock at the bottom to launch and switch apps. Notification banners and mongdock Search in the middle of the screen, all set up with clicks.

What it does

Menu bar

The current app's menus and common shortcuts, sound, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery, virtual desktops, and other apps' tray icons, in one line at the top.

Control Center

Screen brightness (laptop screens and external monitors that support it), night light, and power mode, one click away.

Notification banners

Windows notifications as banners at the top right. Click the clock for a list grouped by app; clearing one in mongdock also clears it from the Windows notification center.

mongdock Search

Press Win+Space for a search box in the middle of the screen. Find apps, Windows settings, files and folders, or do quick math.

Dock

Bottom, left, right, or top. Drag to reorder, pick windows from previews, magnification, light and dark themes.

Calendar

Korean public holidays and lunar dates. Paste an iCal address from Google Calendar or Outlook.com to see your events.

Laptops and many setups

Low-battery alerts, camera and microphone indicator, multiple monitors, high-DPI and portrait screens, and a lightweight mode for remote sessions. Korean and English.

All with clicks

Every setting is a click away and applies right away. Clicking the Dock or menu bar doesn't take keyboard focus from the app you're using.

Built to be trusted

Your taskbar comes back

mongdock hides the Windows taskbar only while it's running and restores it when you pause or quit.

Stays on your PC

Notification contents, window titles, and calendar addresses are used only on your PC. Usage statistics are sent only if you agree when first asked. Privacy policy

Clean uninstall

Settings → About → "Uninstall mongdock…" restores the taskbar and notification sound, then opens the Windows uninstall page.

Good to know

  • Apps that use their own pop-ups instead of Windows notifications, such as KakaoTalk, don't appear as banners. The red dot on their Dock icon still shows.
  • After you change the notification sound for the first time, sign out and back in for it to take effect.
  • Naver Calendar can't be connected because it doesn't offer an iCal subscription address.
  • On Windows 10, rounded corners and some icons may look different from Windows 11.
  • x64 PCs only.

FAQ

I can't see the taskbar.

mongdock hides the Windows taskbar while it's running. Turn off Settings → General → "Hide Windows taskbar", or click the mongdock icon in the notification area once to pause. If mongdock quit unexpectedly and the taskbar is still hidden, start mongdock again, or sign out and back in.

Anything to do before uninstalling?

Go to Settings → About → "Uninstall mongdock…". It restores the taskbar, auto-hide, and notification sound, and opens the Windows uninstall page.

KakaoTalk notifications don't show as banners.

KakaoTalk uses its own pop-ups instead of Windows notifications, so mongdock can't show them as banners. The red dot on its Dock icon still appears.

Can I connect Naver Calendar?

Personal Naver Calendar doesn't offer an iCal subscription address. For Google Calendar or Outlook.com, copy the iCal address and click Settings → Calendar → "Add from clipboard".

Can I use it on a work PC?

It installs and runs without administrator rights, but company policies may block hiding the taskbar, starting at sign-in, or downloading calendars. Check your company's rules first.

It feels slow over remote desktop.

Turn on Settings → General → "Lightweight mode" to switch off animations and blur.

What happens when the trial ends?

After 15 days, the Dock and menu bar stop and the Windows taskbar comes back. Buy mongdock to pick up where you left off, with your settings intact.
